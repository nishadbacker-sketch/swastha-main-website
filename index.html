GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is the initial assessment free?

Yes. This page is for requesting a free initial assessment visit. Our team can explain the scope of the assessment when confirming your appointment.

Does this booking confirm my appointment immediately?

No. Your WhatsApp message is an appointment request. The clinic team will check availability and confirm your date and time.

What should we bring?

You may bring recent school work or relevant reports if available. They are optional; the team will guide you if anything else is needed.

Will my child receive a diagnosis?

This is an educational screening and guidance process, not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for a standardised clinical evaluation.
